# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Tango is a small open-source project. Everyone who participates in it, whether by reporting issues, submitting code, or asking questions, should be able to do so without being treated poorly.

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behaviour

Be respectful when disagreeing with someone. Critique ideas, not people. Be patient with contributors who are learning. Give and accept constructive feedback without making it personal.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following will not be tolerated: personal attacks or insults, harassment of any kind, publishing private information about someone without consent, and any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it by opening a private issue or contacting the maintainer directly. All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly.

## Enforcement

The project maintainer has the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this code of conduct, and to temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behaviour is harmful.

This code of conduct applies to all project spaces including the GitHub repository, issues, pull requests, and any other forums associated with the project.
